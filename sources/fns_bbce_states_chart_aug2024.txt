                       BROAD-BASED CATEGORICAL ELIGIBILITY

Broad-based categorical eligibility (BBCE) is a policy in which households may become
categorically eligible for SNAP because they qualify for a non-cash Temporary Assistance for
Needy Families (TANF) or State maintenance of effort (MOE) funded benefit. The chart below
shows which States implement BBCE, the programs that confer BBCE, the asset limit of the
TANF/MOE program, and the gross income limit of the TANF/MOE program.

BBCE cannot limit eligibility. Households that are not eligible for the program that confers
categorical eligibility may apply for and receive SNAP under regular program rules. Under
regular program rules, SNAP households with elderly or disabled members do not need to
meet the gross income limit but must meet the net income limit.
